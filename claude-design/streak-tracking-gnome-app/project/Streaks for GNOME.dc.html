5Dark variant — amber accent

The same six views in Adwaita dark. Blue at 13px on a #1e1e20 ground goes muddy and the chart scale collapses into the background, so dark runs on amber instead: #ffa348 for fills, buttons and the top of the chart scale, with dark ink on the fills rather than white. The shade ramp is #4a3a22 → #a8712e → #ffa348, which keeps four legible steps where the blue ramp had two. Missed days are #8e4a50, unconfirmed days are hollow with a #6b5230 outline. Streak dots move to the dark Adwaita hues: green #8ff0a4, purple #dc8add, yellow #f9f06b.

4Desktop window — journal removed

Same app, desktop shape. An AdwNavigationSplitView: a 280px sidebar holding Today and the streak list, a content pane that swaps between the day's check-ins 4a and a streak's history 4b, and a split header bar. The extra width buys two things the narrow layout couldn't have: check-in cards two-up, and a chart that shows about seven months at once instead of five weeks. Catch-up 4c and streak creation 4d are AdwDialogs over the window. Journal is gone — no third view, no per-day notes, no recording, and preferences 4e loses its whole storage section. The check-in view switcher at the bottom goes with it; the sidebar is now the only navigation.
